# Textures of the ATRIUM hall

`marble-diffuse.webp`, `marble-rough.webp` and `marble-normal.webp` are the 1K maps of Marble 01
by Rob Tuytel on Poly Haven (https://polyhaven.com/a/marble_01), CC0 (https://polyhaven.com/license),
converted to WebP. The scene samples them triplanar over walls, piers and vault and draws the ashlar
joints on top, so the museum model needs no UVs for its stone.
