# Textures of the ATRIUM hall

`marble-diffuse.webp`, `marble-rough.webp` and `marble-normal.webp` are the 1K maps of Marble 01
by Rob Tuytel on Poly Haven (https://polyhaven.com/a/marble_01), CC0 (https://polyhaven.com/license),
converted to WebP. The scene samples them triplanar over walls, piers and vault and draws the ashlar
joints on top, so the museum model needs no UVs for its stone.

`wood-diffuse.webp` (1K) and `wood-rough.webp` (512) are the colour and roughness maps of Dark Wood
on Poly Haven (https://polyhaven.com/a/dark_wood), CC0, converted to WebP; the scene samples the
colour and the roughness triplanar over everything in wood: the cabinets of the far room, the door,
the bench and the lectern.

`marble-beige-diffuse.webp` (1K), `marble-beige-normal.webp` (1K) and `marble-beige-rough.webp` (512)
are the colour, normal (GL) and roughness maps of Marble 014 on ambientCG
(https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Marble014), CC0 (https://ambientcg.com/license), converted to WebP;
the scene samples them triplanar over the walls and piers in slabs of 5.5 m, so the veins span metres.

`fabric-diffuse.webp` and `fabric-normal.webp` (512) are the colour and normal maps of Fabric 061 on
ambientCG (https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Fabric061), CC0, converted to WebP; the scene dyes the weave
crimson for the bench cushion.
